# QuadletLens 0.1.12

- Adds evidence-scoped exact generic `PodmanArgs` forms for `--interactive`, `--tty`,
  `--privileged`, and `--privileged=false` across Podman 5.4.0 through 6.0.2. This is generated
  command-text evidence only, not runtime or cross-format equivalence; see the
  [capability model](../capability-model.md) and [generator matrix](../generator-matrix.md).
- Adds container `LogDriver`/`LogOpt` and `IP`/`IP6`/`NetworkAlias` native keys.
- Adds network `Driver`, `Options`, `Internal`, `IPv6`, IPAM, and `Label` native keys.
- Adds volume `Driver`, `Options`, `Label`, `Device`, `Type`, and `Copy` native keys.
- Adds exact capability and dry-run generator evidence for all 20 recorded Podman releases from
  5.4.0 through 6.0.2; network-label bare tokens change at 5.6.0 and volume options stop
  requiring `Device` at 6.0.0.
